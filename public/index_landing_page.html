Each concept starts from what you already know and adds one idea. No term appears without being explained, and anything held back for a later concept is flagged where you would expect to meet it.

Work through these in order. Unlike the twelve problems, these concepts are sequential — each one uses the ones before it. Concept 8 draws on all seven that come before, and starting in the middle will leave gaps.

Every concept ends with ten questions that explain both the right answer and the wrong ones, so read the feedback either way. Interview answers are worth saying aloud before opening them.
